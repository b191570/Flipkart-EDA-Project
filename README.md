## Flipkart Customer Service Satisfaction Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes Flipkart customer service data to understand key factors influencing customer satisfaction (CSAT Score). The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in customer interactions, response time, agent performance, and issue categories to improve service quality and user experience.

## Objective
- Identify key drivers affecting customer satisfaction (CSAT Score)
- Analyze impact of response time, issue category, and agent performance
- Detect patterns in customer complaints and service efficiency
- Provide actionable insights to improve customer experience and retention

## Dataset
- 85,000+ customer service records  
- 20 features including:
  - Customer interactions (category, sub-category)
  - Agent details (agent, manager, shift, tenure)
  - Time-based data (issue reported, response time)
  - Customer satisfaction (CSAT Score)

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Data Visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly)
- Machine Learning (Scikit-learn)
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Analysis Performed
- Cleaned and processed 85K+ records by handling missing values and formatting inconsistencies
- Engineered features such as **response time** and encoded categorical variables
- Conducted **Univariate, Bivariate, and Multivariate Analysis (UBM)**
- Built 15+ visualizations to analyze trends across categories, agents, and time
- Evaluated relationships between CSAT Score and operational factors

## Key Insights
- Over **60% of customers gave a 5-star CSAT rating**, indicating generally high satisfaction levels  
- **Returns (51%) and Order-related issues (27%)** account for ~75% of total customer complaints  
- **Response time is a critical factor** — delays significantly reduce customer satisfaction  
- **Agent performance varies significantly**, with top agents achieving consistently higher CSAT scores  
- **No strong correlation between item price and customer satisfaction**, indicating service quality matters more than product cost  
- Customer satisfaction showed a **declining trend from 2022 to 2023**, highlighting potential service gaps  

## Visualizations
- CSAT Score distribution
- Response time vs satisfaction analysis
- Agent performance comparison
- Category-wise complaint distribution
- Time-based trend analysis
- Correlation heatmap & pair plots

## Business Recommendations
- Reduce response time through process optimization and automation  
- Focus on resolving **returns and order-related issues**, which drive majority of complaints  
- Identify and train underperforming agents to improve service quality  
- Implement performance-based incentives for high-performing agents  
- Improve support during peak periods to maintain consistent service quality  

## Conclusion
This analysis highlights that **service efficiency, response time, and issue resolution quality** are the primary drivers of customer satisfaction. By addressing operational bottlenecks and improving agent performance, Flipkart can significantly enhance customer experience and retention.
